**App name** (max 30): LifeDesk: Bills & ID Reminders

**Short description** (max 80):
Snap any bill, ID or policy and never miss a payment, renewal or expiry again.

**Full description:**
LifeDesk keeps track of everything in your life that has a date, payment or deadline.

📸 SNAP IT, DONE
Photograph a bill, Emirates ID, passport, visa, insurance policy, tenancy contract, warranty or receipt, or share a PDF or email. On-device AI extracts dates, amounts, providers and reference numbers, and creates reminders automatically. Tenancy cheques become one reminder per payment.

🔔 REMINDED AT THE RIGHT TIME
Pop-up reminders days ahead for bills and months ahead for passports, IDs and visas. Mark paid or snooze right from the notification.

🎙️ HANDS-FREE VOICE
“Hey Google, open LifeDesk Voice”, then say “remind me to pay DEWA 450 on Friday” or “mark credit card as paid”. LifeDesk updates your list and answers out loud, without opening the app. Also from the Quick Settings tile, the widget or your headset button.

🤖 AI BRIEFING & ASSISTANT
A daily briefing of what matters, read aloud on request. Ask “when does my passport expire?” or “how much do I spend on subscriptions?”.

💸 MONEY RADAR
12-month cash-flow forecast, spending by category, price-increase alerts and forgotten subscriptions.

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Documents are processed on your phone. Optional account with Gmail/email for private cloud backup. App lock with fingerprint. Free, supported by a small banner ad.

Also: home-screen widget, calendar export, JSON backup, dark futuristic interface.

# LifeDesk Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 30 September 2026_

LifeDesk helps you track bills, documents, renewals, warranties and subscriptions. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## What stays on your phone
- **Photos and PDFs you scan** are read on your device with on-device text recognition (Google ML Kit). They are never uploaded.
- **Your reminders** (titles, dates, amounts, providers, reference numbers) are stored in a private database on your phone.
- **Voice commands** are turned into text by your phone's speech-recognition service (for example Google). LifeDesk only receives the resulting text and does not record audio.

## What is sent to the cloud (only if you sign in)
If you create an account or continue with Google, LifeDesk uses **Google Firebase** to:
- sign you in (your email address and a user ID), send verification and password-reset emails, and
- back up your reminder list to a private record that only your account can read (Firestore security rules enforce this).

Document photos are **not** included in cloud backups. You can use LifeDesk without an account; nothing is then sent anywhere.

## What we don't do
- No advertising, no ad IDs, no tracking or analytics SDKs.
- We do not sell or share your data with anyone.

## Permissions
- **Notifications:** to remind you before due dates.
- **Camera** (through the system camera/photo picker): to scan documents you choose.
- **Internet:** only for sign-in and optional cloud backup.
- **Biometrics:** optional app lock.

## Deleting your data
- **On your phone:** Settings → *Delete all data*, or uninstall the app.
- **In the cloud:** Settings → *Delete account* permanently removes your account and its backup.

## Children
LifeDesk is not directed at children under 13.
